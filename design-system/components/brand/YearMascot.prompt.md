Mascot of a school year — a small friendly logo that follows the cursor and hides the AMDG easter egg. Put it on every surface tied to a year.

```jsx
<YearMascot year={3} />                 // Navicella, 48px
<YearMascot year={4} size={34} />       // Bussolina on a card
<YearMascot year={1} size={72} speak={false} />
```

- 1 Semino · 2 Ichthy · 3 Navicella · 4 Bussolina · 5 Terra
- Click → greeting, then year-themed lines.
- In plain-HTML artefacts use `assets/artefatti/lab-artefatto.js` with `<body data-anno="N">` — same figure, same behaviour.
